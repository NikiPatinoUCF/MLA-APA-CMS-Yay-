How to Play Edit Quest

Your guide to mastering MLA, APA, and CMS style conventions

🎮 Getting Started

Edit Quest is an interactive educational game that helps you master citation and style conventions through fun, gamified challenges. Here's how to begin your journey:

  1. Choose a Style Guide: Select from MLA, APA, or Chicago Manual of Style
  2. Pick a Region: Each style guide has themed challenge areas like Comma Castle or MLA Mountain
  3. Select Difficulty: Start with Beginner and progress through Intermediate to Expert levels
  4. Answer Questions: Complete challenges to earn points, badges, and unlock new levels
  5. Track Progress: View your achievements and rank on your Profile page

📝 Challenge Types

Edit Quest features multiple types of interactive challenges:

☑️ Multiple Choice

💎 Scoring System

Earning Points

  • Base Points: 10-50 points per correct answer depending on difficulty
  • Streak Bonus: 1.5x multiplier for 3+ consecutive correct answers
  • Time Bonus: Up to 20 extra points for answering within 10 seconds

Difficulty Levels

  • Beginner: 10-20 points per question
  • Intermediate: 25-35 points per question
  • Expert: 40-50 points per question

🏆 Ranks and Badges

Style Ranks

Progress through four ranks based on your total points:

Rank Points Required
🎓 Novice Editor 0 points
🛡️ Grammar Guardian 500 points
📜 Citation Sage 1,500 points
👑 Master Stylist 3,000 points

Badge Collection

Earn badges by:

  • Completing levels at each difficulty tier
  • Achieving perfect runs without errors
  • Building long answer streaks
  • Answering questions quickly
  • Mastering all three style guides

💡 Tips for Success

  • Read Carefully: Pay attention to details in questions and answer options
  • Learn from Mistakes: Review explanations after each answer to understand the rules
  • Build Streaks: Focus on accuracy to maintain streaks and earn bonus points
  • Practice Regularly: Return often to reinforce your knowledge
  • Complete All Guides: Master MLA, APA, and CMS to unlock the Final Boss Level
